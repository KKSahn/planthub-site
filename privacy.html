Privacy

What we collect

From growers who make an account: name, email, a password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), a phone number if you give one, your nursery's name, city and what you grow, your listings and your photos.

From visitors: you can browse without an account, and we don't ask who you are. We count visits using Cloudflare's analytics, which uses no cookies. Like any website, the services that deliver these pages see your device's internet address when they do (listed below).

Why

To run your account, show your listings, and send the emails the site needs: confirming your address and resetting your password. If you email us, we use your address to answer you.

Who sees what

Anyone can see your nursery's name, city, state and what you grow, and each listing with its quantity, price, photos and the date you last confirmed it. These pages are public and search engines may show them. Your nursery stays private until you give it a city.

Only you can see your email and phone number on the site. They are never shown to visitors or other growers.

Before a photo is sent, the site shrinks it, which also removes what the camera stored in the file, such as where it was taken.

Who helps us run the site

Supabase holds accounts, listings and photos, on servers in the United States. Cloudflare serves the site and handles our email address. Resend delivers the site's emails. The pages also load their fonts from Google Fonts, and code libraries from jsDelivr and Tailwind's content network. We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.

Deleting your information

Sign in, open your account page and press "Delete this account". Your account, nursery, listings and photos are removed straight away, and can't be brought back. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll do it for you.
